ClearFrame downloads (but does not commit) the official portable Real-ESRGAN ncnn Vulkan release and its general-purpose `realesrgan-x4plus` model at setup time.

- [Real-ESRGAN](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN) is distributed under BSD-3-Clause. See the upstream repository's `LICENSE` file for its complete terms.
- [Real-ESRGAN ncnn Vulkan](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN-ncnn-vulkan) is distributed under MIT. See the upstream repository's `LICENSE` file for its complete terms.

The installer extracts only `realesrgan-x4plus.bin`, `realesrgan-x4plus.param`, and the portable executable into the local runtime folder. It does not extract any anime model.
